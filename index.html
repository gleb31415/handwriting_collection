English handwriting collection: every word twice

You will write 100 English words twice, in two blocks:

  • Fast: the way you write for yourself, in notes or at a lecture. Do not try hard, do not correct.
  • Neat: as if another person has to read it. Your own handwriting, just more careful; no block letters.

Write in cursive or joined-up handwriting if that is how you normally write; otherwise your usual print. Write with a stylus. One attempt per word; if your hand slips, press “Clear” and write it again. Takes about 25–30 minutes. You can stop at any time: progress is saved in this browser.

Continue from an exported file (another device or browser). The participant code, block order and all written words are restored; writing continues from the first missing word.

Export

If the file does not download (this happens in some embedded viewers), press “Copy” and paste the text into a message or a note.
